04

Why Paper

Paper is the ImageNet of deformable manipulation

Infinite free data

Every page turn is a labeled trajectory. Glossy, matte, thick, thin, old, new. Success is binary — no annotation needed.

Physics transfer

Paper bends, doesn’t stretch. Same thin-sheet mechanics govern envelopes, labels, flex circuits, surgical membranes, and packaging.

No benchmark exists

SoftGym benchmarks cloth and rope. There is no equivalent for thin-sheet. d4m-paper-v1 fills that gap.

Lin et al., “SoftGym: Benchmarking Deep RL for Deformable Object Manipulation,” CoRL 2020 — covers cloth, rope, fluid. No thin-sheet tasks.
